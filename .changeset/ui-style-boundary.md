Component rules are no longer in a cascade layer: they are scoped to `.workflow-builder-root` at build time with enough weight to beat host resets (Bootstrap's reboot, Tailwind's preflight, `@tailwindcss/forms`, classless CSS), and `PortalContainerProvider` / `usePortalContainer` render popups and overlays inside a themed container. Design tokens follow the current design system (new run-status, code, node icon tile and badge tokens; `--wb-ds-canvas-edge-stroke-bold` and `-critical` removed, `-regular` is 1px), and the date picker's selected and today's dates are readable in both themes.

Migration: wrap standalone components in `.workflow-builder-root` with `data-wb-theme` (the wrapper paints the theme's surface and text colour; `--wb-public-root-background-color` and `--wb-public-root-color` change that); move `--wb-*` overrides from `:root` to `.workflow-builder-root`; the defaults layer is now `wb-ui.base`, so rename any `ui.base` / `ui.component` in your own `@layer` statements; rename overrides of the chip, run-status and badge tokens, which now come in `bg` / `fg` pairs: `--wb-ds-components-chips-solid` / `-solid-text` / `-inset-outline` become `-bg-solid` / `-fg-solid` / `-fg-outline`, `--wb-ds-canvas-run-status-icon-*` becomes `-fg-*`, and `--wb-ds-components-badge-text-*` / `-icon-*` merge into `-fg-default` / `-fg-inverse`. See the [Upgrade to 4.0 guide](https://www.workflowbuilder.io/docs/get-started/upgrade-to-4/).
