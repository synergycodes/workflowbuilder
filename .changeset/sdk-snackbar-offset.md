The editor's snackbar stack can be moved with `--wb-public-snackbar-offset-bottom` (distance from the viewport bottom to the lowest snackbar, default 20px) and `--wb-public-snackbar-offset-x` (horizontal shift from the centre, default 0px), for example to keep snackbars above a docked panel. The defaults keep today's position.
